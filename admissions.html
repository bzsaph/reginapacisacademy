Questions

Which classes can my child join?

Nursery — Baby, Middle and Top class — and Primary One to Primary Five.

What language will my child learn in?

The school is francophone and anglophone: French and English are used at every level.

Do you offer boarding?

No. Regina Pacis Academy is a day school — every child goes home at the end of the day.

Is there a charge for the school bus?

No. Comfortable transport is provided for all the children, free of charge. Call the office to check that your area is covered.

What is the 20% reduction?

The first 100 children enrolled receive a twenty per cent reduction for the whole year. Ask for the registration number you are given when you enrol.

Can my nursery child stay later?

Yes. Nursery children who need to stay are cared for, and go home accompanied by their older brothers and sisters at 17h.
