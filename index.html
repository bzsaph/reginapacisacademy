About the school

Instruire pour Construire Admissions →

Regina Pacis Academy is a nursery and primary school in Jali, Gasabo District, Kigali. Our motto — Instruire pour Construire — is what we are here to do: teach children so that they can build. The school crest carries a second promise, Une bagage pour l'avenir: a foundation for the future.

The school teaches in French and in English at every level, from the Baby class through to Primary Five, and every child travels to and from school on our own buses at no cost to the family.

🗣️

Francophone & anglophone

Lessons in French and in English at every level, with educators devoted to the children in their care.

🏫

New, comfortable buildings

Recently built classrooms, and a balanced menu for the children each day.

🌳

Room to flourish

Space for your children to play, to grow and to become themselves — lieux d'épanouissement.

🚌

Transport gratuit — free for every child

Comfortable school buses bring every child to school and take them home again, at no cost to families.
